Uncompromising Commitment to Health, Safety & Environment

Safety is Not a Policy. It's Our Culture.

At RHQ Services, our most important asset is our people. Ensuring their health and safety, and that of our clients and the public, is the foundation upon which we build everything. Our goal is simple and absolute: zero incidents. This is not just a target; it is a mindset embedded in every employee, every process, and every project we undertake.

Comprehensive Training

Every team member undergoes rigorous, ongoing safety training, including site-specific orientations, hazard recognition, and emergency response procedures. Competency is regularly tested and verified.

Robust Safety Policies

Our Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) Management System is COR certified and exceeds industry standards. It governs all aspects of our operations, from job planning to execution and review.

Environmental Protection

We are dedicated stewards of the environment. Our procedures for spill prevention, emissions control, and land reclamation are designed to minimize our footprint and protect Alberta's natural beauty.

Our Zero-Incident Philosophy

We believe that all incidents are preventable. This philosophy is supported by a multi-layered approach to safety:

  • Proactive Hazard Assessment: Before any work begins, our teams conduct thorough Field Level Hazard Assessments (FLHAs) to identify and mitigate potential risks.
  • Leadership Commitment: Management leads by example, actively participating in safety audits, site inspections, and incident investigations.
  • Employee Empowerment: Every employee has the right and responsibility to stop any work they deem unsafe without fear of reprisal. We encourage open communication and a 'see something, say something' culture.
  • Continuous Improvement: We analyze near-misses and incidents to understand root causes and implement corrective actions across the entire company, ensuring we are always learning and improving our safety performance.
