Our History – Building Alberta's Future

From Humble Beginnings to Industry Leader

RHQ Services was founded over two decades ago with a single bulldozer and a powerful vision: to provide unparalleled earthworks services to Alberta's growing industries. Our founder, a second-generation earthmover, recognized the need for a contractor that combined a deep respect for the land with an unwavering commitment to operational excellence and safety. From those early days working on small-scale civil projects around Calgary, our reputation for reliability and quality work began to grow.

The turn of the millennium marked a significant turning point. As activity in the Alberta oil sands began to accelerate, RHQ Services was there. We were one of the first contractors to specialize in large-scale overburden removal and site preparation in the Fort McMurray region. This period of rapid growth tested our capabilities and solidified our core values. We invested heavily in our people and our fleet, expanding our equipment roster to include the massive haul trucks and shovels required for world-class mining operations. Our commitment to local communities deepened, as we prioritized hiring locally and partnering with Indigenous-owned businesses.

Expansion and Diversification

Our success in the oil sands provided a foundation for strategic expansion. We leveraged our expertise to serve other sectors, including hard rock mining, infrastructure development, and environmental reclamation. We opened regional offices in Edmonton and Grande Prairie to better serve our clients across the province, ensuring that our project management and support teams were always close to the action. This expansion was driven by our clients' trust in our ability to manage complex, multi-faceted projects from conception to completion.

Key milestones in our journey include the completion of major tailings dam construction, the successful reclamation of several legacy mine sites ahead of schedule, and achieving our Certificate of Recognition (COR) for our health and safety program. Each milestone is a testament to the dedication of our team and our continuous pursuit of improvement. Today, RHQ Services stands as a testament to the power of hard work, integrity, and a forward-thinking approach. We are proud of our history in building Alberta's future and are excited to continue contributing to the province's prosperity for decades to come.
